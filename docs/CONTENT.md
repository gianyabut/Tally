# Tally — Content & Copy Guidelines

## Voice
Terse, factual, ledger-like. No exclamation points, no emoji, no "please". The interface is an instrument, not a mascot.

## Two registers — never mixed in one element
1. **System/meta**: IBM Plex Mono, UPPERCASE, letter-spaced, dot separators. Examples: TIMELINE·2026 · T−72D · STEP 2 OF 3 · FY2026 · WORKED FULL DAY · ⎘ shift_0831.jpg · NO NOTIFICATIONS — ALL CLEAR
2. **Human**: IBM Plex Sans, sentence case. Examples: "How are you starting?" · "a day off, spend anytime" · "teammates see the dates, not the note"

## Rules
- All numbers, dates, deltas, filenames: mono, always.
- Dates: MM-DD in lists (08-31), ranges with en dash (07-07–08), detail form "2026-11-30 · MON · REGULAR".
- Deltas always signed + unit: IL +1.0 · VL −2.0 · OT +1.0. **OT in days only — money never appears.**
- Vocabulary (fixed): "ledger" not dashboard · "log" not submit · "file a leave" not request · VL / SL / In-Lieu (IL) / Unpaid / OT day · "proof" not attachment.
- Toasts = what happened + consequence: "Proof attached — IL credited" · "You joined Bluefin Studio — ledger moved into the team" · "3 invites sent — pending until they join".
- Signal red is reserved for user-action-needed; adjacent copy must name the action: "● PROOF MISSING — ADD TO CREDIT".
- Consent copy verbatim wherever data visibility changes:
  - "Your ledger moves with you — balances become visible to its Admin, Manager and HR."
  - "teammates see the dates, not the note"
  - "it's your ledger — no approvals"
- Invite email must carry inviter name + team name prominently and a quiet "Ignore this if you don't know them."
- Empty/quiet states stay mono-faint, factual: "NO NOTIFICATIONS — ALL CLEAR".
